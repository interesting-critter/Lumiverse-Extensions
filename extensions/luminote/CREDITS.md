# Luminote credits

## Original creator

**Coffee** — the original creator of Luminote and its Obsidian-style Lumiverse workspace experience. Coffee is a member of the Lumiverse Discord server. She made this extension before it was prepared for publication; Luminote is released with her original authorship acknowledged. She was, by her own legend, **2 shy to upload it to GitHub — or possibly even to have one.**

## Publisher

**Prolix OCs** — release publisher and maintainer.

## Required release attribution

Use the following wording in extension listings, release notes, and announcements:

> Luminote was originally created by Coffee from the Lumiverse Discord server and is published by Prolix OCs.

Do not remove or obscure Coffee's original-creator credit from redistributed copies of Luminote.
